On AI assistance. These articles are written and edited with AI support, and so is most of the work they describe. What that does not extend to is the substance: the measurements are mine, and every figure, claim and structural decision here I checked myself.

It is deliberate rather than incidental. Producing documentation this way has turned out to be one of the most valuable skills of the past year, and a site about running an agent on your own hardware would be an odd place to hide it.

Some paragraphs and wordings may read as slop — more redundant, or more elaborately constructed, than you would have written them. That is a choice too: where they give the text a character of its own without changing what it says, I kept them.
