{%- comment -%} Local override of the theme's read_time.html. The base figure is the theme's own — words over 135 — but it is shown on a ladder rather than to the minute. A reading time is derived from an assumed words-per-minute, and every reader's own rate differs, so "62 mins" claims a resolution the estimate does not have. under 15 minutes 5, 10, 15 15 and over 15, 30, 45, 60, ... Nearest step, half up, and deliberately not floored: a floor shows 29 minutes as 15, which misleads in the direction that costs the reader something. `divided_by` on integers is integer division, so adding half a step before dividing is what rounds rather than truncates. {%- endcomment -%} {%- assign words = include.content | number_of_words -%} {%- assign mins = words | divided_by: 135 -%} {%- if mins < 15 -%} {%- assign bumped = mins | plus: 2 -%} {%- assign shown = bumped | divided_by: 5 | times: 5 -%} {%- else -%} {%- assign bumped = mins | plus: 7 -%} {%- assign shown = bumped | divided_by: 15 | times: 15 -%} {%- endif -%} {%- if shown == 0 -%}{%- assign shown = 5 -%}{%- endif -%} {{ shown }} mins read
